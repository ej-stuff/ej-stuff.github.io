613 FIGHTS · WHICH GUN ACTUALLY WINS

The Armory

Not accuracy, but results: when a gun did most of the work in a fight, how often did we win that fight? One main (AR or LMG) and one long gun (DMR or SR) per player, plus every SMG.

D282 & S821 · Duo FPP · 613 decisive fights · 310 matches · 2 Jun – 6 Oct 2026

Each fight is credited to the player who did the most damage in it, and to the gun he used most in that fight. Win% = wins ÷ all decisive fights that gun led; trades and losses both count against it. We opened = we landed the first hit. Guns with fewer than 5 fights are left out, except in the SMG tables. Anything under ~15 fights is a hint, not a result.

The obvious catch: a DMR mostly fights at range, where we see people first. An LMG mostly fights up close, where ambushes happen. So each section also checks the gun in fights we opened versus fights where we were ambushed.

No clear main: M249, AK-M and HK416 all win about a quarter

The AK-M (26.1% of 23), M249 (25.0% of 36) and HK416 (23.1% of 26) are within three points of each other. The M249 is still the one he carries most, and wins 38% of the fights he opens with it. The HK416 is odd: poor when he opens (2 wins in 13) but his best gun when ambushed (4 in 13). Reading his fights, the M249 shows up in many of his best moments: point-blank knocks and kills after S821 is down (the chicken dinner on 22 Sep ended with an M249 knock at 13m and a kill at 7m on 17 HP).

Ambushed, the M249 and AK-M are both about 1 win in 13. For those two, who hits first decides the fight more than the gun. The G36C (3 wins in 5, three of those fights from 6 October) and AUG A3 look good but have only 5 fights each. Avoid the RPD (1 win in 7).

The previous version said "no LMGs, drop the RPD". That came from data that missed our kills. Many of S821's RPD and M249 fights end with him killing the last enemy after D282 went down, and those were counted as losses. With kills counted, his main guns sit between 38% and 50% (the Beryl is 5 of 7 and the M16A4 1 of 5, both small samples).

Among the common ones the HK416 wins most (48% of 21, 70% when he opens). The ACE32 is his most-used gun (37 fights, 38%) and the one behind most of his close-range clutches in the fight review (e.g. two kills at 10m and 3m on 2 HP on 9 Aug). The RPD still loses the damage race badly (−49.8 per fight). It wins by finishing, not by trading.

M24 or Mini 14

The M24 is still his best individual weapon on the whole site: 55% accuracy, 298 damage per combat-minute, 78 kills per combat-hour. Most of his headshot knocks in the fight review come from it (157m, 173m, 185m, 210m, 236m). The M24 has now caught up with the Mini 14: both win 50% of the fights they lead. Either is a good pick. The FAL (SLR) is a solid third.

The Mk12 is his worst long gun (5 wins in 15). It's the same "hurts but doesn't finish" pattern as for D282.

Method

  • Source: all 613 decisive duo fights (including the first 5 minutes) from 310 matches, 2 Jun – 6 Oct 2026. Individual accuracy/damage numbers come from the Weapon Stats data.
  • Credit: each fight goes to the player who did the most damage in it and his most-fired gun. A gun gets credit or blame for fights it led, not only fights where it got the kill.
  • Win: nobody on our side went down, or every enemy in the fight was killed and nobody on our side died. Numbers include a September fix that added kills the old data missed.
  • Samples are shown next to every number. Under ~15 fights, treat it as "try more", not "switch".
  • Correlations from our own games, not a controlled test.
