529 FIGHTS · WHICH GUN ACTUALLY WINS

The Armory

Not accuracy, but results: when a gun did most of the work in a fight, how often did we win that fight? One main (AR or LMG) and one long gun (DMR or SR) per player, plus every SMG.

D282 & S821 · Duo FPP · 529 decisive fights · 285 matches · 2 Jun – 23 Sep 2026

Each fight is credited to the player who did the most damage in it, and to the gun he used most in that fight. Win% = wins ÷ all decisive fights that gun led; trades and losses both count against it. We opened = we landed the first hit. Guns with fewer than 5 fights are left out, except in the SMG tables. Anything under ~15 fights is a hint, not a result.

The obvious catch: a DMR mostly fights at range, where we see people first. An LMG mostly fights up close, where ambushes happen. So each section also checks the gun in fights we opened versus fights where we were ambushed.

M249 is now his best main — narrowly

Last refresh the HK416 was the pick. On more fights it dropped to 12.0%, and only 1 of 12 fights D282 opened with it was won. The M249 is his most-used gun and converts a third of the fights he opens with it. Reading his fights, the M249 shows up in many of his best moments: point-blank knocks and kills after S821 is down (the chicken dinner on 22 Sep ended with an M249 knock at 13m and a kill at 7m on 17 HP).

The gaps are small and every option is bad when he's ambushed (M249 1 win in 16, AK-M 0 in 11). What decides his close-range fights is who hits first, not the gun. Avoid the RPD.

ACE32 as the default, HK416 or Beryl when they're there

The ACE32 is his most-used gun and the one behind most of his close-range clutches in the fight review (e.g. two kills at 10m and 3m on 2 HP on 9 Aug). It holds up when ambushed (21%) better than anything else he carries often. The HK416 and Beryl win more per fight on smaller samples. The Beryl is now 5 wins in 6. Take them when they drop.

RPD: drop it. 20 fights, 2 wins, 0 of 11 when ambushed, worst damage difference on the page.

M24 stays, Mini 14 has caught up

The M24 is still his best individual weapon on the whole site: 53% accuracy, 288 damage per combat-minute, 79 kills per combat-hour. Most of his headshot knocks in the fight review come from it (157m, 173m, 185m, 210m, 236m). The Mini 14 now wins more fights per fight (50% vs 36%, 64% when he opens) on a smaller sample. Either is a good pick. The FAL (SLR) is a solid third.

The Mk12 is his worst long gun (1 win in 10) — same "hurts but doesn't finish" pattern as for D282.

  • Correlations from our own games, not a controlled test.
