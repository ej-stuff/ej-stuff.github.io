01

The Drop

The biggest change in how you play happens in the first 60 seconds on the ground.

Every Erangel landing

Every dot is one of your landings on Erangel. The bigger the dot, the more enemies were within 250 metres of you during your first minute on the ground.

The map shows Erangel only, because it's where you play most. Every number in this chapter counts all maps.

June: into the crowd

On average 6.9 enemies landed within 250 m of you. In 35% of games it was 8 or more. Games that started like that averaged 31st place.

July – October: drifting out

The dots slide away from the big towns. By September it was 2.4 enemies per landing, and only 1 game in 50 dropped into a crowd of 8+. October slipped back a bit (3.6, and 11% crowded drops). Keep that month in mind: it shows up again in Chapter 07.

November: the quiet corners

2.0 enemies per landing, and not a single crowded drop. A third of your landings had nobody within 250 m at all. Tonight's chicken dinner started from one of them.

02

The Quiet Minutes

A quiet landing buys you time. Here's what you did with it.

Time from landing to your first fight
1.6 minutes

In June, the first shot (yours or theirs) came a median 1.6 minutes after you touched down. In 41% of games you were fighting inside the first minute.

5.3 minutes

In November, only 12% of games had a fight in the first minute, and in 46% nobody shot at you for six minutes or more. You loot, you get kitted, then you go looking.

The payoff. Games where the first fight came 6+ minutes after landing averaged 11th place and a 51% top-10 rate. Fights in the first minute: 30th place, 16%.

Part of that runs the other way: a fight in the first minute is often the one you die in. But that's the point of landing quietly.

Tonight's win, 10 November: nobody within 250 m at landing, and the first shot came 13 minutes after you touched down. Three of your four chicken dinners started like this.

03

The Reach

The fights moved further away, and the guns followed.

27 m → 61 m

The median distance of your hits more than doubled. The share of hits from beyond 100 m went from 14% to 29%.

What you carry changed with it. SMGs did 12% of your damage in June and 4% in November. The LMG phase peaked in August (26% of your damage) and has all but gone (4%).

DMRs went from 32% to 49%: half your damage now comes from a marksman rifle, mostly the Mini 14.

And you shoot first. You landed the first hit in 46% of fights in June and 62% in November. At 60+ metres you usually see them before they see you.

Who does the damage is shifting too: S821's share went from 54% in June to 61–64% since October.

04

Living On The Edge

You stopped running to the middle of the circle.

Where you stood relative to the next zone

Each dot is a game, placed by where you were relative to the next safe zone (the white circle) during phases 2–4. The bright ring is the edge of the white circle. Inside it you're already safe; outside it you still have to rotate.

June: inside

Mostly inside or close to the next circle. You spent 6% of your time alive in the blue.

November: on the rim

59% of the time outside the next circle and 14% in the blue. On Erangel this month it was 27%. You rotate late and along the zone, not through the middle.

Does it hurt? No. In games that lasted 15+ minutes, spending 5–15% of the time in the blue had the best top-5 rate of all (50%), better than never touching it (34%). Above 15% it drops back to 45%.

The edge is a style now, not a mistake. Just don't live in the blue.

05

The Leash

One thing went the wrong way.

31 m → 38 m

On foot you were a median 31 m apart in June, and more than 50 m apart a third of the time. In November it was 38 m, and 46% of the time.

It costs you late. In games that lasted 15+ minutes and where you were 50 m+ apart for less than half the time, you finished top-5 in 43–44%. Apart for more than half the time: 7% (1 game in 14).

Tonight's chicken dinner: a median of 17 m apart.

06

The Toolbelt

Smokes, frags and heals: the quiet upgrade.

Per game, June to November: smokes 0.9 → 1.5, frags 0.7 → 1.1, heals 2.1 → 3.5.

You live longer now, so some of that is just more time to use them. But smokes per 10 minutes alive rose too, from 0.8 to 1.0.

Smoke is a winning habit. In games that lasted 15+ minutes, throwing 3+ smokes went with a 60% top-5 rate and an 88% top-10 rate. No smokes: 24% and 47%.

All four chicken dinners had smokes in them: 3, 6, 1 and 4.

07

Did It Pay Off?

Yes. And the months where you slipped back prove it.

The line is your 20-game rolling average placement. Up is better. Stars are chicken dinners. The background bands are months.

June to November: top-10 rate 27% → 42%, top-5 13% → 25%, average survival 11.2 → 15.3 minutes, damage per game 240 → 320.

The proof

Each dot is a month. Further left means quieter landings; higher up means more top-10s.

Your three quietest months (November, September, July) are your three best. Your three hottest (June, August, October) are your three worst. When October's drops got hotter again, the results went straight back to June levels.

What Still Costs You

Fresh from the per-game data, not repeated from the other pages.

Spreading out

46%

The share of time you were 50 m+ apart in November, the highest of any month. Late in the game that's the difference between a 43% and a 7% top-5 rate (Chapter 05).

The October relapse

27%

October's top-10 rate, back at June's level, in the month the drops got hot again (3.6 enemies at landing, 11% crowded). The style only works when you stick to it.

Fights end harder now

46%

The share of November's fights you lost outright, up from 41% in June, even though you also win more of them (36% vs 31%). There are far fewer trades: fights now go one way or the other. The Engagement Report shows which ones you lose.

The damage is moving to S821

61%

S821's share of your damage in November (54% in June, 64% in October). D282 opens more fights at long range but finishes fewer. That's fine as a role split, as long as it's a choice.

The text was written on 10 November. The charts and the explorer are drawn from stats-data/evolution.json, which the pipeline rebuilds after every refresh.
  • Games: Duo FPP only. Every metric is computed per game from the position, event and circle data behind Match Analysis, then averaged by month.
  • Enemies near the landing: distinct enemy players within 250 m of where you landed during your first 60 s on foot.
  • First fight: the first time either of you hit, or was hit by, an enemy player with a weapon or throwable, counted from landing.
  • Hit range: distance from the shooter to the target for every hit you landed (under 800 m).
  • Zone: position relative to the white circle (the next safe zone) every ~10 s while alive. "In the blue" uses the game's own flag.
  • Apart: distance between you two when both are alive and on foot.
  • Late-game comparisons only use games that lasted 15+ minutes, so they don't just measure who died early. These are patterns from your own games, not experiments.
