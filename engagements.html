The Engagement Report

Every mid- and late-game fight, written out second by second and read one at a time. What we keep getting wrong, what keeps working, and what to change next session.

D282 & S821 · Duo FPP · 285 matches · 2 Jun – 23 Sep 2026

Each fight was turned into a timeline: where both of us were, how far apart, every shot, hit, knock, revive, grenade, heal and vehicle entry, from 60 seconds before the first hit to 60 seconds after the last. I read all 379 fights that start after the 5-minute mark, noted what decided each one, then counted how often each pattern happens across all of them.

Across those 379 fights: 77 won, 112 traded, 190 lost (20.3% win rate). A win means we downed at least one enemy and nobody on our side went down. A trade means both sides lost someone. Links open the match on the map.

Stop doing

1. Getting caught alone. One of us goes down with the other 40m+ away in 68 fights. It usually starts with pushing a knock or walking ahead.

2. Driving into teams. We're in a vehicle in the 10 seconds before 58 fights. 53% of those are losses, and several were over in under 3 seconds.

3. Staying where the sniper can see you. The second bolt-action shot usually lands on the same spot a few seconds after the first.

4. Reviving in the line of fire. Smoke doesn't fix it. Smoked revives were re-knocked just as often.

5. Fighting from below. 32 fights with the enemy 10m+ higher: 3 wins.

6. Hanging around after a win. Third teams arrive while we bandage in the same spot.

Keep doing

1. Opening with a knock. S821 did it 49 times and only one of those fights was lost. D282 did it 28 times.

2. Both shooting the same player. 36% win and 11% loss, against 18% and 57% otherwise. It only happens in 1 fight in 7.

3. Shooting teams in or leaving their car. 14 fights: 6 wins, 8 trades, no losses.

4. Third-partying. Loss rate drops to 35% (from 51%) when the first enemy we hit was already fighting someone else.

5. Healing before the push, and the clutches. Several of our best fights come from this. See Chapter 03.

02

What Goes Wrong

In rough order of how many fights it costs. Each has real examples from the list.

68 fights · S821 37 · D282 31

Knocked with the teammate 40m+ away

The most common single way we lose fights. The typical case: one of us knocks someone and runs to finish him, or walks ahead through open ground. His teammate is still up and kills whoever arrives. A quarter of all fights (91) also start with us more than 50m apart, and those end in a loss 58% of the time against 48% otherwise.

  • 7 Aug · Erangel — final two teams, S821 alone. FAL knock at 71m, then ran to 23m to finish him; the knocked player's teammate killed S821 with an M416. Finished 2nd.
  • 7 Jul · Karakin — final two teams. S821 took A1 to 8 HP at 127m, then walked forward alone (70–78m from D282) and ate an AUG headshot at 10m. D282 lost the 1v2. Finished 2nd.
  • 3 Jun · Miramar — S821 knocked A1 with a Mk12 from 45m, then walked 70m away from D282 to finish him. MP5K from the second enemy at 3m.
  • 3 Jun · Taego — D282 opened with an 87-damage knock at 40m. S821 ran in to 11m alone and died. D282 then killed both anyway.
  • 5 Jun · Karakin — 170–207m apart. Both died to different teams, 50 seconds apart.
  • 3 Jun · Vikendi — top 4. D282 walked 62m ahead of S821 in the open; M416 headshot knock at 37m. S821 smoked four times but couldn't get to him.
  • D282's other versions: 22 Sep · Paramo (walked into an RPD at 11m, S821 133m away), 5 Sep · Erangel, 20 Jun · Erangel.

Instead: max 40m apart while anyone could be around. A knock is not a kill — the enemy's teammate is the threat. Finish from cover, or push together, or leave him and hold the angle.

58 fights start in a vehicle · 53% lost

Driving into teams and bailing in the open

The pattern: drive straight at or past a team we can see, stop 15–60m from them, get out in the open. Or get shot in the car, then hop in and out while they reload. A lot of these have zero damage dealt.

  • 30 Aug · Erangel — drove at 26 m/s into an AK + M249 pair. Both dead in the car in 2 seconds. 0 dealt.
  • 2 Jun · Taego — stopped the UAZ 44m from a Groza player in the open, 3m apart. S821 knocked instantly, one grenade killed D282 and finished S821. 9 seconds.
  • 3 Jun · Miramar — drove past a Mk12 + M24 pair at 216m with S821 on 31 HP. Knocked in the car, then 100 seconds in the open at 230–300m. 0 dealt, 348 taken.
  • 12 Sep · Erangel, 25 Aug · Erangel, 30 Aug · Erangel, 6 Jul · Miramar — car stopped 15–40m from a team we'd driven at. All losses.
  • 22 Sep · Taego — drove the UAZ at a pair; one PanzerFaust rocket killed both.
  • 7 Jul · Miramar / 7 Jul · Miramar — D282 in and out of the BRDM 3 times, then 6 times, in sight of enemies. Kar98k knock at 289m, then AUG knock at 33m.
  • Separate cars arriving one at a time: 22 Jun · Erangel, 12 Sep · Erangel, 20 Jun · Vikendi, 22 Sep · Erangel.

Instead: stop 150–300m short, behind a hill or building, and walk in. If shot at in the car, keep driving until out of sight — don't bail in the open. One car, one decision: whoever drives decides in or out.

67 fights with a bolt-action one-shot on us

Staying in the sniper's sightline

One knock from a Kar98k, M24, AWM or L6 is often unavoidable. The second one isn't. Again and again the same sniper hits the other player, or the same player after the revive, because nobody moved after the first shot.

  • 28 Jun · Erangel — one Kar98k player killed both of us with 4 shots in 12 seconds at 128–147m. Nobody moved or smoked until the last shot.
  • 5 Sep · Deston — standing 1m apart in the open, M24 headshot on D282 at 205m, then on S821 3 seconds later.
  • 20 Jun · Erangel — good smoke-revive after a Kar98k knock. 60 seconds later S821 was headshot at 103m by the same player from the same direction.
  • 20 Jun · Vikendi to 20 Jun · Vikendi — one game, four M24 hits on us at 467m, 177m, 77m and 124m, two of them right after getting out of the car in open ground. Finished 2nd.
  • 7 Jul · Erangel — re-peeked the same angle against two M24s. Both dead in 8 seconds.

Instead: after the first sniper hit, the next thing either of you does is get out of that line — behind something solid, not a smoke, and not healing in place. Never re-peek the angle you were just shot from.

61 revives in fights · 18 re-knocked within 15 s

Reviving in the line of fire

We revive a lot, and usually fast. Almost one revive in three ends with one of us down again within 15 seconds. Smoke doesn't change that: 6 of 18 smoked revives were re-knocked, against 12 of 43 without smoke. The problem is where the revive happens.

  • 31 Aug · Vikendi — revived twice in the same spot, in a VSS's sightline at 77m. S821 was knocked three times, then killed.
  • 31 Aug · Erangel — revive in a Kar98k's line at 90m; the reviver was knocked 2 seconds later.
  • 25 Aug · Miramar — revived S821 with the enemy 4m away and still up. He knocked S821 again 17 seconds later.
  • 23 Sep · Erangel — top 3. Revived under three incoming grenades. One frag re-knocked S821 and did 65 to D282.
  • 31 Aug · Erangel — revived inside a BZ-grenade area; the zone killed both.
  • Revives that worked: 5 Jun · Vikendi (2 smokes, sniper 200m away, then shield + heal), 17 Jun · Erangel (smoke, revive, then D282 killed the AWM player at 5m), 5 Jun · Miramar (revive behind the vehicle, both healed, then S821 knocked the next team).

Instead: drag or wait until you're behind hard cover, or kill/relocate the shooter first. A knocked teammate can crawl — move him, not yourself, into the open.

32 fights · 3 wins · 66% lost

Fighting from the low ground

When the enemy is 10m or more above us at first contact, we almost never win. The worst ones are long duels from below and leaving good high ground to rotate down.

  • 9 Aug · Taego — Dragunov duel at 130m against a Dragunov and a Kar98k 17–21m above us. Two headshots on D282, one on S821. 439 damage taken.
  • 31 Aug · Paramo — killed a pair from 38m high ground (great), then rotated down into the valley under two teams on higher ground. Both dead.
  • 5 Sep · Deston, 5 Sep · Deston, 5 Sep · Deston — three fights in one game all from 12–25m below.
  • 24 Aug · Erangel — top 5. D282 pushed uphill to 11m into an RPD.

Instead: if they're above you, don't trade shots. Break line and reposition, or leave. When we have the high ground, stay on it until the fight is done.

seen over and over while reading

Staying put after winning a fight

After a kill we often heal or loot in the same spot, walk into the open still hurt, or push straight into the next team. Third teams heard the fight.

  • 17 Jun · Erangel — S821 killed both with the Vector, then we bandaged for 60 seconds 4–7m from their building. Saiga team pushed, both dead.
  • 29 Jun · Miramar — top 4, 586 damage dealt. After the last kill we stood 3m apart, S821 on 10 HP. The last enemy killed both with a Vector.
  • 16 Aug · Erangel — after a knock and kill, stayed next to the building: frags and a jerrycan fire.
  • 3 Jun · Miramar — clean 120-second long-range win for 0 damage taken, then S821 pushed downhill into a fresh team. Both dead.
  • 20 Jun · Erangel — killed one of a pair, nobody watched the second. He walked up and killed both.

Instead: after a kill, first say how many are left. Then heal behind cover or move 50–100m before healing. Loot only what you need.

When our first hit knocks that player within 10 seconds, we almost never lose. S821 landed 49 of these (1 loss), D282 28 (5 losses). Long-range headshots do a lot of the work.

  • 28 Jun · Deston — S821 ACE32 headshot kill at 10m while on 28 HP, then Dragunov knock at 67m. 300 dealt.

  • 17 Jun · Erangel — final fight, chicken dinner. D282 M249 headshot knock at 28m, S821 ACE32 kill at 30m in the same second.
  • 29 Jun · Erangel — chicken-dinner game, top 5. D282 M249 98 at 14m, S821 ACE32 102 at 6m. 279–0.
  • 22 Sep · Taego — S821 Beryl headshot at 42m and D282 Mk12 headshot at 76m in the same second. 289–0.
  • 22 Sep · Taego — D282 Mini14 knock at 71m, S821 M16A4 kill at 26m, 5 seconds. 176–0.

05

Next Session

1. Stay within 40 metres

Don't finish a knock alone while his teammate is up. Push together or hold the angle.

2. Park 150–300m short, behind cover

Never drive at a team you can see. Shot in the car? Drive out of sight, don't bail.

3. Sniper hit = move

Both of you out of that line before anyone heals. Don't re-peek the same angle.

4. Revive behind hard cover only

Smoke is not cover. Deal with the shooter or move first.

5. Keep the high ground

Don't duel from below. Don't leave good ground to rotate down past teams.

6. After a kill: count, heal behind cover, move

Say how many are left. Don't bandage 5m from their building.

7. Open together, on the same player

Call the target, both shoot it. When a car stops near you, shoot the car.

How This Was Made

  • Data: raw PUBG telemetry for 285 duo matches (2 Jun – 23 Sep 2026). Solo, team deathmatch and arcade modes are excluded.
  • A fight = all combat involving either of us, split when 45 seconds pass without any. 529 decisive fights; 379 start after 5:00 and are the ones read and counted here.
  • Reading: each fight was written out as a timeline (positions every ~10 s, distance between us, height, every shot burst, hit, knock, kill, revive, throwable, heal, boost and vehicle entry/exit, blue-zone ticks) and read one by one.
  • Counting: the patterns were then tagged automatically across all 379 fights. "Knocked alone" = one of us knocked with the other 40m+ away. "Sniper one-shot" = a single Kar98k/M24/AWM/Mosin/Win94/L6 hit of 60+ that downed us. "Low ground" = enemy 10m+ higher at first contact. "Opening knock" = our first hit's target downed within 10 s. "Same player" = both of us hit the same enemy within 5 s.
  • Win/trade/loss: a win means nobody on our side went down, so tags like "sniper one-shot" can't have wins by definition. For those, the count is the useful number.
  • These are patterns from our own games, not experiments. Examples were picked by hand while reading.
